# Class 01 — Build Your First Personal Agent in 30 Minutes

A hands-on class for **non-technical** people. By the end, every attendee has a working agent on their own laptop that knows who they are, can look at one of their real accounts, and does one job for them on a schedule.

## What's in this folder

| File | Who it's for | What it's for |
|---|---|---|
| [`training-script.md`](training-script.md) | Trainer | The full run of show: what to say, what to click, checkpoints, and what to do when something breaks live |
| [`scenarios.md`](scenarios.md) | Trainer + attendees | Six ready-made agent ideas — pick one to demo, attendees pick one to build |
| [`starter-kits/`](starter-kits/) | Attendees | Copy-paste-ready folders for the three main scenarios: instructions, memory, progress, and skills already filled in |

| [`handout.md`](handout.md) | Attendees | One page to keep: the four pieces, the three files, the prompt formula, the first-week plan |

## The idea the whole class hangs on

Every personal agent, no matter how fancy, is the same four things:

| Piece | Plain-English | What it is in Claude |
|---|---|---|
| 🗂️ **A desk** | Where it works and keeps its papers | A folder on your computer (the *workspace*) |
| 📓 **A notebook** | What it knows about you and what it was doing | Three text files: `CLAUDE.md`, `memory.md`, `progress.md` |
| 🤝 **Hands** | What it can see and touch in your real life | *Connectors* — Gmail, Calendar, Drive, Notion… |
| ⏰ **An alarm clock** | When it works without you asking | A *scheduled task* (on your laptop) or a *routine* (in the cloud) |

Optional fifth piece once the first four work: 📋 **recipes** — *skills*, saved step-by-step procedures it follows the same way every time.

If attendees leave remembering only this table, the class worked.

## Before you run it

1. Read the **"Verify before you present"** box at the top of `training-script.md` and click through it on your own machine — menu names in Claude Desktop move around between releases.
2. Send attendees the **pre-work** list in the script at least a day ahead. Installing and signing in eats 10 minutes of a 30-minute class if you let it happen in the room.
3. Copy one starter kit to your desktop as a fallback, already set up, in case a live demo stalls.
